    How to play

    Assign each army to a human or a bot in New match. Humans share this device and take turns. Choose 6-bot demo to watch a full match. On your turn, choose one of your pieces, then a highlighted destination.

    Use Online to host a room, join by invite or code, or watch a match.

    Each army’s last move has a dashed origin outline and a solid destination outline.

    The camera starts at the center, facing your army or the opening army in a demo. Click the board to focus. Use WASD to move, Space to rise, and Shift to descend. Drag to look around.

    In Layers, choose an axis and depth to highlight a cross section of the 3D board. The view snaps to a slice along the active army’s direction of advance. Only destinations on the highlighted slice are shown. Use Pieces and Destinations to make selections by coordinate.

    The board

    An 8 × 8 × 8 core meets six centered 4 × 4 × 4 home volumes. Each army starts with its back rank on the outermost plane and 16 pawns one plane inward.

    Piece movement

    Rook
    Slides along one axis.
    Bishop
    Slides diagonally along two or three axes, taking equal steps on each.
    Queen
    Slides along one axis or a two-axis diagonal.
    Knight
    Jumps two cells along one axis and one along a second.
    King
    Steps to any of the 26 neighboring cells.
    Pawn
    Steps one cell straight ahead into an empty cell. On its first move, it may advance two cells if both are empty. Captures one layer ahead in any of the eight cells surrounding the straight-ahead cell.

    Each army’s pawns advance inward along its face axis. A pawn that crosses the midpoint plane on that axis promotes to a queen, rook, bishop, or knight. The midpoint lies between layers 3 and 4 and passes through the central 2 × 2 × 2 block.

    Hills and checkmate

    Each army’s hill is the four gold cells on its back rank that start with its king, queen, and two rooks. Move your king safely onto a rival hill to eliminate that army. Your moves must keep your king out of check.

    An army is checkmated if its king is in check when its turn begins and it has no legal move. The last army standing wins.

    Turns follow North, East, South, West, Zenith, and Nadir. Armies with no legal move and no check are skipped. The match is a draw if every remaining army is blocked.

    Scoring

    Eliminating an army by claiming its hill or delivering checkmate earns 20 points. Capturing a piece earns its value.
